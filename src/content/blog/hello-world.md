The blog is live! This is what a post looks like.

## How posts work

Every post is a Markdown file in `src/content/blog/`. At the top of the file is a little block of info (called frontmatter) that looks like this:

```md
---
title: "My post title"
date: 2026-10-26
tags: ["prog", "savage"]
---
```

You can invent **any tags you want** — they'll automatically get their own tag pages and show up on the [tags page](/tags/).

## What you can put in a post

- **Headings** with `##` and `###`
- **Bold**, *italics*, and `inline code`
- Lists, quotes, and links
- Images: `![alt text](/images/my-screenshot.jpg)` — put the actual image in the `public/images/` folder
